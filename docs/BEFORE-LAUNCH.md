## Offers shown by the theme

- "Buy 3 get 1 free" and "Buy 5 get 2 free" are copied from the reference layout. They only show on products of type T-Shirt whose title doesn't say hoodie, jogger, polo, shorts or set.
- **Create the matching automatic discount, or switch the offer off** in Theme settings › Offers & delivery.

## Store data issues found while building

- **Product types are wrong for non-tees:** hoodies, joggers, polos and the shorts set are typed "T-Shirt". This makes the "Product type" filter misleading. Fix: bulk-edit the product type to Hoodie, Joggers, Polo, Shorts or Set.
- **Option names vary:** Color/Colors/Colour, Size/Sizes/T shirt Size, and Fit/Fit Type/Type/Length type. The theme reads all of them. Standardising them still makes the storefront Size/Colour filters cleaner.
- **Colour spellings:** "Nevy blue", "Neavy blue" and "OFF- WHITE" are mapped by the theme. Renaming them to "Navy Blue" and "Off White" would tidy the filters.
- **Compare-at prices:** these show discounts of 50%+ on most products (e.g. AED 65 vs AED 140). The theme shows them exactly as stored.
- **Placeholder stock:** stock numbers in Shopify are placeholders, so "Sold out" states won't be meaningful until real inventory is tracked.
